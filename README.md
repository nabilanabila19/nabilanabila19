## 🌸 About Me

Hi! I'm **Nabila**, a student at **Politeknik Negeri Sriwijaya** and a graduate of **Bangkit Academy 2024** (Mobile Development cohort).

I started out in mobile development, but my curiosity kept pulling me deeper — into **Machine Learning**, **Data Science**, and building **ETL pipelines**. I love working with data, finding patterns, and turning raw information into something meaningful.

- 🔭 Currently finishing my final year project: an Android app for **chili leaf disease detection** using ML 🌶️
- 📊 Exploring the world of data engineering & ML pipelines
- 🌱 Learning more about **TFLite**, **KMP**, and **MLOps**
- 📫 Let's connect on **LinkedIn**!
